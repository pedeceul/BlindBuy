## Product Overview

**BlindBuy** is a Chrome Extension that transforms the OLX shopping experience by adding community-driven review functionality to ads and sellers. Users can now make informed purchasing decisions based on real feedback from other buyers.

### Product Tagline
*"Make informed decisions on OLX with community reviews"*

### Product Description
BlindBuy enhances your OLX shopping experience by adding a comprehensive review system directly to OLX ads. Rate and review both individual ads and sellers, helping the community make better purchasing decisions while building trust in the marketplace.

## Problem Statement

### Current OLX Limitations

1. **No Review System**: OLX lacks a built-in review system for ads or sellers
2. **Limited Trust Indicators**: Users can't verify seller reliability before purchase
3. **No Community Feedback**: No way to share experiences with other buyers
4. **Risk of Scams**: No community warnings about problematic sellers
5. **Incomplete Information**: Price and photos aren't enough for informed decisions

### User Pain Points

- **Uncertainty**: "Is this seller trustworthy?"
- **Risk**: "Will I get scammed?"
- **Lack of Information**: "What's the real condition of this item?"
- **No Community**: "I wish I could see what others experienced"

## Solution

### BlindBuy's Approach

BlindBuy solves these problems by:

1. **Adding Reviews to OLX**: Seamlessly integrates review functionality into existing OLX pages
2. **Community-Driven**: Leverages collective user experiences
3. **Seller Tracking**: Identifies and rates sellers across multiple ads
4. **Real-Time Updates**: Shows current ratings and reviews instantly
5. **Easy Integration**: Works with existing OLX workflow

### How It Works

1. **Install Extension**: One-click installation from Chrome Web Store
2. **Browse OLX**: Navigate to any OLX ad as usual
3. **See Reviews**: View existing reviews and ratings for the ad/seller
4. **Add Your Review**: Rate and comment on your experience
5. **Help Others**: Your reviews help future buyers make decisions
